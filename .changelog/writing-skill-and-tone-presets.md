Agents now write intents, plans, approval and completion notes, and the chat around them from one new skill, `cc-writing`, so they open with the point, connect their ideas, and in Bahasa Indonesia compose rather than translate. Each member can pick a tone with `member tone --preset colleague`, `casual`, `formal`, or `non-technical`, and `member presets` lists them with the line each records. A member with no tone is written for as `colleague`. A tone never reaches `context/` notes, the catalog, or the glossary, which stay English. Existing workspaces need no migration, and a tone you already wrote keeps working.
