An approval or completion note is now written in the record's language, English unless the author's member entry says otherwise, and states the decision: what was approved, the answers to open questions, conditions, and extra asks. It no longer pastes the person's reply, which may be in the language of the conversation. The conversation itself is unchanged: replies, approval confirmations, and summaries stay in the language the person is using.
