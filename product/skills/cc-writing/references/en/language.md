# English

The rules in `SKILL.md` are written for English, so this file adds only what
they leave out.

Start each sentence from something the reader already has and end it on the new
thing. The opening words point back to the sentence before or to something the
reader knows, which is what lets them read without going back. Keep asides out
of the middle of a sentence: a bracket, a nested qualification, or an inserted
definition goes in a sentence of its own.

Introduce three or four new names at most before saying why they matter. A
paragraph that opens with five components, two flags, and a record ID has lost
the reader before it says anything about them.

Hedge once, where the uncertainty actually is, and name what is uncertain:
"I haven't checked whether the old CLI quotes the line" rather than "this should
probably mostly work". A hedge on every line tells the reader nothing.

Address the member by their member ID when the chat names them at all, and write
"you" freely; English needs no honorific. The tone file says how its register
shows in a record and in chat.
