# English, formal

For managers or people outside the team. No contractions and no slang, with
"please confirm" where `colleague` would say "say so". Formal is the same rules
with nothing added: it does not mean longer sentences, passive verbs, or
nominalizations, which would break the rules in `SKILL.md` while sounding
polite.
