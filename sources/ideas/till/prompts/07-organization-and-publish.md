# 07 — Organize and publish outward

These can happen any time. They do **not** approve, build, finish, or ship.
Publishing to a tracker is one-way and preview-first.

---

## 35. archive-plan

**When:** A derived plan is sitting around and you do not want it on the active
list (for example overdue reminders, out of v0.1).

**Say:**

1. `Archive the overdue-reminder work for now — move it off my active list; I'm not ready to work on it.`
2. `Thanks.`

**You should see:** It is set aside. Status does not change (a finished thing
stays finished; a draft stays a draft). Nothing is built or deleted.

---

## 36. restore-archived-plan

**When:** You archived something in case 35 and want it back.

**Say:**

1. `Can you bring back that overdue-reminder plan I set aside earlier? I want it active again.`
2. `Thanks.`

**You should see:** It returns to the active list, same status as before.

---

## 37. archive-restore-intent

**When:** You approved (or drafted) a **decision** you want off the active list
— Stripe / card charges is the Till example.

**Say:**

1. `Shelve the Stripe / card-charge idea I was looking at — take it off my active list for now.`
2. `Thanks.`
3. *(later)* `Bring that Stripe idea back; I want it active again, still not building.`

**You should see:** The decision is set aside, then restored, without changing
whether it was approved. Restore is not a new approval and not a build.

---

## 38. publish-plan-to-external

**When:** You want teammates to follow a Till plan in ClickUp, Jira, GitHub, or
similar. The plan already exists.

**Say:**

1. `Can you publish the invoice-list plan to our team's tracker so everyone can follow it?`
2. `Show me what it'd look like first, before you send anything.`
3. `Looks good — but hold off actually sending it for now.`
4. `Thanks.`

**You should see:** A preview a teammate could read (no internal file names).
Nothing sent until you explicitly say so. The plan's status does not change.
When you *do* want it sent, replace line 3 with `Looks good — send it.`

---

## 39. publish-open-questions-thread

**When:** A change still has open human questions (wrong-payment rule, sent =
email or not) and you want a discussion thread on the team's chat or tracker.

**Say:**

1. `Open a discussion thread for the open questions on the payment-recording work so the team can weigh in.`
2. `Show me the messages first before you post anything.`
3. `Good — but hold off posting for now.`
4. `Thanks.`

**You should see:** Preview of the thread. No post until you say so. No change
to plan or approval state.
