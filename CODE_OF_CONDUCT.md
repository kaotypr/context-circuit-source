# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Everyone taking part in this project — issues, pull requests, reviews, and
discussion — is expected to make it a place where people can do good work.

Expected:

- Assume the other person is trying to get it right.
- Critique the change, not the person who wrote it.
- Say what you mean plainly, including disagreement, and give reasons.
- Accept that a maintainer decision may go against you, and move on.

Not accepted:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning remarks about anyone.
- Discrimination or unwelcome attention on any basis, including who someone is,
  where they are from, what they believe, or how they work.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop.

This applies in every project space, and to public behavior when someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers by opening a GitHub Security
Advisory or a private message to the repository owner. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the reporter is not identified to the person reported without
their agreement.

Maintainers respond in proportion to what happened: a clarification, a warning, a
removed comment, or a block from the project. A maintainer who is the subject of a
report takes no part in deciding it.
