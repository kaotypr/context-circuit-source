# Intention — i032-deliver-positive-scenario-suite

_Status: draft, waiting for your approval._

## Intention

Deliver the first **nine positive Agent Harness journeys** across fresh projects,
context gathering, and intent-to-verification work. Every journey will run in a
new real host session against its own complete temporary condition.

Results will be graded from observable workspace, Git, lifecycle, child-role,
context-usage, and host evidence. Human-facing meaning may use bounded semantic
grading, but it cannot override contradictory mechanical evidence.

## Expectations

- The suite covers all nine agreed successful journeys in three families.
- Every scenario is independent and declares its complete starting condition.
- Mechanical outcomes are evaluated deterministically from captured evidence.
- Human-facing communication is graded by meaning rather than exact wording.
- Context efficiency and role boundaries are required for every scenario pass.
- Results clearly distinguish a product failure, an inconclusive observation,
  and a harness failure.
- A questioned run preserves its workspace and provides a practical reproduction
  path.

## The plans

1. **Deliver the nine positive real-host scenarios and their grader.**
   _After this:_ maintainers can run the agreed product journeys, inspect their
   evidence, compare hosts and revisions, and reproduce any questioned result.

## How carefully this is checked

**`Standard`**

Explanations:
- **Explore:** you check it yourself as you work alongside the agent — no separate
  verification. Meant for trying things out.
- **Standard:** a second, independent agent verifies the finished work against your
  definition of done before it's called done.
- **Critical:** the strictest — independent verification plus a repair-and-recheck
  loop. For anything risky or impossible to undo (money, security, production, data
  you can't get back).
