## Intended behavior

At most one active writer per execution is enforced through a simple atomic
ownership lock; a competing writer receives a read-only or blocked result and a
live lock is never silently stolen. Recovery inspection reports interrupted
executions, and resume is eligible only when plan revision, configured anchor
branches and captured anchor commits, worktree paths, and ownership still match.
On failure or interruption, branches, worktrees, commits, handoffs, and records
are preserved and never silently cleaned up.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Add the one-writer lock (execution id, owner/session id, repository set,
acquired timestamp, current attempt, recovery state), recovery-inspect, resume
eligibility, and preservation/cleanup safety checks to
`wrapper/runtime/engine.sh`. Records are written atomically so a partial record
cannot grant ownership, resume a writer, or permit completion.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: an execution directory and the requesting session's identity. Outputs: a
lock acquire/deny result, a recovery-inspection report, a resume-eligibility
verdict, and preserved evidence on interruption.
