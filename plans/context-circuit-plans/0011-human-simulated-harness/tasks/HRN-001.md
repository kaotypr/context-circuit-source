## Intended behavior

A source-only maintainer human-simulator role plays an ordinary person who has
never heard of Context Circuit, workspaces, plans, or the runtime, and drives the
real product coordinator through natural conversation. The coordinator is spawned
as a sub-agent inside a fresh isolated workspace instantiated from the assembled
template, with no scenario metadata, expectations, or any signal that it is under
test, so the observation reflects the true product experience.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `.claude/agents/cc-human-simulator.md` as the Claude Code spawnable
definition of the host-neutral persona contract: it receives only a case's
`human:` block (persona, turns, reactions, visible_expectations), speaks only in
lay goal-and-reaction language, may not read workspace files/contracts/runtime or
inspect state, and reports a per-expectation conversational verdict. Under
`template-harness/human`, define the coordinator-under-test spawn contract: launch
the real product coordinator (from the instantiated workspace's `AGENTS.md`,
`WORKFLOW.md`, skills, and `engine.sh`) with its working directory set to the
isolated workspace and no test awareness injected. The human-simulator is a test
actor, not a fourth product role, and no `cc-*` product skill is added for it.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the v0.5 human-simulator and coordinator-under-test role designs.
Outputs: `.claude/agents/cc-human-simulator.md` and the coordinator spawn
contract under `template-harness/human`.

## Risks and open questions

- The persona must stay ignorant of internals for the whole run; leaking product
  vocabulary or rescuing the coordinator invalidates the observation.
- The coordinator must receive no injected context that reveals the test, or
  HRN-AC-01 fails.
