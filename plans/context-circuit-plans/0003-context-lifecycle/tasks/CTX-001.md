## Intended behavior

Product Knowledge is described by three contract schemas. The context index is a
retrieval catalog carrying stable ids, summaries, domains, keywords, freshness,
and provenance — retrieval metadata, not a copy of page bodies. A proposal
records provenance and an explicit impact status before acceptance, and the
impact shape drives completion reconciliation without silently accepting
knowledge.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `wrapper/contracts/schemas/context-index.yaml` as the retrieval catalog
shape: per-unit page path, context id, topic, summary, keywords/aliases, related
domains and repositories, decisions and constraints covered, status, owner,
freshness, and primary provenance (INV-KNOWLEDGE-01). Author
`wrapper/contracts/schemas/context-proposal.yaml` as the proposal record shape:
target unit, add/change/retire/stale operation, statement, evidence references,
affected repositories and commits, confidence, provenance, and impact status
(INV-KNOWLEDGE-02). Author `wrapper/contracts/schemas/context-impact.yaml` as the
completion-reconciliation impact shape with the status flow
not-assessed -> review-needed -> accepted / deferred / conflict, plus
no-update-needed.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the v0.5 context-lifecycle model. Outputs: three schema files — the
retrieval catalog shape, the proposal record shape, and the context-impact shape.
