## Intended behavior

The writer brief's fact-bearing sections are a fixed template filled by
deterministic slot substitution from the grounding manifest and the plan, not
coordinator free-composition (INV-GROUND-03). The template carries a required
repository-grounding slot with a directive to read and honor the discovered
guidance — listing every discovered skill with its description so the writer picks
by relevance (zero-miss), and rendering an explicit "no guidance discovered" form
when the manifest is empty. A preflight refuses to deliver a brief whose grounding
slot is missing or unfilled — a contract violation, not a silent omission.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author the fixed brief template at `wrapper/adapters/writer-brief.md` with slots
for worktree, environment, the required grounding directive, plan snapshot,
Product Knowledge, allowed paths, verification, and the single coordinator-authored
task-focus line. In `wrapper/runtime/engine.sh` implement the deterministic
slot-fill from the manifest + `plan.yaml` and the preflight check that verifies the
assembled brief contains the required grounding slot before the writer is
delivered.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the grounding manifest, `plan.yaml` (scope, verification, allowed paths),
and the coordinator's one-line task focus. Outputs: an assembled writer brief with
the grounding section rendered from the manifest (full skill menu or the empty
form), and a preflight pass/stop result.
