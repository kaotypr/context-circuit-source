## Intended behavior

The source repository draws an explicit release boundary that classifies every
path as shipped machinery, mutable seed, or never-ship, so assembly copies only
template-owned files and generic product behavior. A blank universal project
workspace seed under `template/` becomes the initial files a user receives, and
an upgrade replaces only the machinery while preserving everything the user
creates (identity, Product Knowledge, plans, and bindings).

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author the `release_boundary` in `wrapper/manifest.yaml` with the three
classifications (shipped / mutable_seed / never_ship), making the manifest the
single owner of the boundary. Author the blank workspace seed under `template/`
with its root `.gitignore` excluding at least `/repositories/`,
`/repositories.local.yaml`, and `/.runtime/`. List the staged shipped inventory
in `scripts/release-manifest.txt`, including the source-only exclusions. The
seed carries generic workspace structure (README, AGENTS.md, WORKFLOW.md,
workspace.yaml placeholder, context/, sources/, plans/), never one project's
Product Knowledge.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the source/template/workspace three-identity model and the assembly
boundary from the v0.5 design. Outputs: the `release_boundary` classification in
`wrapper/manifest.yaml`, the blank seed tree under `template/` with its
`.gitignore`, and the staged inventory in `scripts/release-manifest.txt`.

## Risks and open questions

- The manifest must classify every path; an unclassified path is neither
  shipped nor preserved and breaks REL-AC-01.
- The seed must not import source Product Knowledge, plans, `.runtime/`, or
  bindings as if they were installed product files.
