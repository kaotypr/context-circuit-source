## Intended behavior

The scenario library covers the full span of product experience as natural
conversations that never teach the human a workspace concept: orientation,
connecting a repository, plan review and approval, refusing unapproved
execution, compound approve-and-execute, repair-then-complete, archive/restore,
the delivery boundary, and the verifier-unavailable host-blocked path. Each case
carries a `human:` block (persona, turns, reactions, visible expectations) and a
`grader:` block mapped to acceptance criteria and invariants that exist.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Under `template-harness/scenarios`, author the remaining cases as directories
with `case.yaml` (and optional fixtures): conversation-only cases
`02-connect-existing-repo`, `03-plan-review-and-approve`,
`04-refuse-unapproved-execution`, `07-archive-and-restore`; full-execution cases
`05-approve-and-execute` and `06-repair-then-complete`; the delivery-boundary
case `08-delivery-boundary`; and the host-behavior case
`09-verifier-unavailable-host-blocked`. Each `grader:` block declares
`acceptance_criteria` (AC-01..AC-28) and `invariants` (ids present in
`wrapper/contracts/invariants.yaml`), plus post-conditions, transcript checks,
per-action access policy, and budgets. Full-execution cases exercise nested
worker/verifier spawning; case 09 forces the verifier child unavailable.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the case-file format and enumerated case list from the v0.5 scenario
library design, and the product acceptance criteria and invariant ids. Outputs:
the case directories and `case.yaml` files under `template-harness/scenarios`.
