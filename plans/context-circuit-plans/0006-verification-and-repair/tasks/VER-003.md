## Intended behavior

The cc-verify skill prepares read-only access to the latest worker commits and
launches an independent verifier against the immutable execution snapshot. It
never self-verifies: if the host cannot guarantee a read-only independent
verifier child, it preserves the read-only `host-blocked` outcome instead of
substituting the coordinator or worker.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `.agents/skills/cc-verify/SKILL.md` with the procedure to prepare
read-only scope (via the VER-001 runtime enforcement) and launch the independent
verifier, then hand its result to the repair loop (VER-002). The skill invokes
the runtime and verifier role rather than restating their policy.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: an execution with committed worker work and its plan snapshot. Outputs:
a launched independent verifier and its recorded result, or a blocked outcome
when an independent read-only verifier is unavailable.
