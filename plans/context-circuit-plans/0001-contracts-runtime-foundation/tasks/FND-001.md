## Intended behavior

The product has a single authoritative catalog of invariants where every product
rule resolves to exactly one owning file, so no skill or role file redefines a
rule that another file already owns. The authority order and conversational
contract are fixed in WORKFLOW.md, the safety spine and host-adapter surface are
fixed in AGENTS.md, and a canonical internal-to-plain-language projection lets
the coordinator speak in project terms without exposing internal mechanism.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Write `wrapper/contracts/invariants.yaml` as the invariant catalog and one-rule-one-owner
map. Define the authority order and conversational contract in
`wrapper/adapters/WORKFLOW.md`. Write the safety spine and provider-neutral
host-adapter surface in `wrapper/adapters/AGENTS.md`, including that host
evidence is observation-only and never authorizes a route, role, gate, or
completion. Fix the internal-term to plain-language projection in
`docs/terminology.md` and align `context/TERMINOLOGY.md` as the source-side
vocabulary. Host evidence lives in the bounded `host_evidence` shape and never
authorizes work (INV-HOST-01).

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the v0.5 design for agent behavior, terminology, and the source/template
split. Outputs: `invariants.yaml` owner map, `WORKFLOW.md` authority order and
conversational contract, `AGENTS.md` safety spine and host-adapter surface, and
the `docs/terminology.md` plain-language projection.
