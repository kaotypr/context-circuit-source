## Intended behavior

The runner executes one scenario case end to end: it assembles the same
`context-circuit-template` artifact a user would receive, instantiates an
isolated temporary workspace, records a read-only pristine baseline snapshot,
applies the case's fixtures, then drives the human-simulator/coordinator turn
loop. The case file splits into a `human:` block and a `grader:` block so the
persona cannot see the mechanism, and a run captures the transcript, the
file-access trace, and telemetry into a disposable per-run directory.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Implement `template-harness/human/run-scenario.sh` to assemble via
`scripts/release-artifact.sh` into a temp dir, copy it into a fresh isolated
workspace (importing no source Product Knowledge, plans, `.runtime/`, or
bindings), snapshot the baseline (at least `context/` and `workspace.yaml`),
create `setup.repositories` and `setup.sources` fixtures, spawn the coordinator
and human-simulator, and drive the turn protocol while writing transcript,
file-access trace, and usage/timing telemetry under a gitignored per-run
directory. Under `template-harness/scenarios`, author the `case.yaml`
`human:`/`grader:` schema, the `scenarios/` layout and README, and the anchor
case `01-new-project-simple-idea` as a fully worked example.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the assembled template artifact, a `case.yaml` (human + grader blocks),
and the chosen product host. Outputs: an isolated workspace, a baseline
snapshot, and the per-run transcript, file-access trace, and telemetry
artifacts.
