## Intended behavior

The three execution roles — coordinator, writer, and verifier — have fixed,
non-overlapping boundaries that hold across Codex, Claude, and Cursor. Those
hosts act only as transports carrying provider-neutral host evidence. Shipped
skills are read-as-procedure packets resolved solely by path, with no
host-specific skill directory shipped.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Define the coordinator, writer, and verifier role boundaries in
`agents/coordinator.md`, `agents/writer.md`, and `agents/verifier.md`: the
coordinator is the conversational root and never a child execution role; the
writer is the sole writer for one execution; the verifier is independent and
read-only. In `wrapper/adapters/AGENTS.md`, bind the three hosts as transports
whose observed identity/version/capabilities/role/permission-mode live in the
bounded `host_evidence` shape and never authorize a route or gate. Establish
that a shipped skill is resolved only by path (INV-SKILL-01) at
`.agents/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`, with no host-specific skill dir shipping.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the safety spine and invariant owners from FND-001. Outputs: three role
definition files with non-overlapping boundaries and the host-adapter binding
plus skill-packet convention recorded in `AGENTS.md`.
