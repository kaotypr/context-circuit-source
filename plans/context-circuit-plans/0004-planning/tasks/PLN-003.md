## Intended behavior

The cc-plan skill defines two procedures. Create retrieves grounding by the
request's concepts, domains, repositories, decisions, and constraints, expands
the request into a grounded draft, and validates it via the runtime; missing or
contradictory information becomes an open question, never an invention. Review
is an interactive, non-executing plan discussion that reports risks and gaps
without editing status, approving, or executing the plan.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `.agents/skills/cc-plan/SKILL.md` with the create procedure (brief,
grounding retrieval, drift check, validation) and the review procedure (the
review checklist from the design). It invokes the runtime operations from
PLN-002 rather than restating policy owned by the invariants.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: a natural-language request plus accepted Product Knowledge. Outputs: a
validated draft plan (create) and a risks-and-gaps discussion (review) that
leaves status unchanged.
