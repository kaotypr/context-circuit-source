## Intended behavior

plan.yaml is the single canonical owner of the stable plan id, title,
objective, human status, repository map, context references, task ids and
dependencies, allowed paths, and acceptance/verification identifiers. The task
schema is a synchronized projection of that canonical data, never a second
lifecycle: task files may carry rationale and implementation notes but must not
own status or duplicate canonical identifiers. Readable PLAN.md and per-task
templates make the plan understandable without reading plan.yaml.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `wrapper/contracts/schemas/plan.yaml` (schema_version 1) covering
repositories, context grounding, and the task list. Author
`wrapper/contracts/schemas/task.yaml` as the projection shape that reuses the
plan's identifiers rather than redefining lifecycle. Ship the readable
`docs/templates/plan.md` and `docs/templates/task.md` templates that mirror the
readable-plan and task-design requirements.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the planning-and-execution design (readable plan requirements, machine
plan requirements, task design). Outputs: two schema files and two markdown
templates that together fix the plan/task contract.
