## Intended behavior

The cc-archive skill maps human "archive" and "restore" language to the exact
runtime moves from ARC-001. It never traverses the archive during normal
orientation and communicates that archiving is a pure organizational move that
implies no completion, delivery, cleanup, or deletion.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `.agents/skills/cc-archive/SKILL.md` so it invokes the `plan_archive` and
`plan_restore` primitives in `wrapper/runtime/engine.sh` and reports the outcome
in project terms. The skill owns only the conversational mapping; it adds no
status gate or validation, which the runtime deliberately omits for these moves.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: a human request to archive or restore a named plan. Outputs: the exact
archive/restore move performed via the runtime, a plain-language confirmation,
and orientation that continues to read only the active plan area.
