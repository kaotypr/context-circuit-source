## Intended behavior

A path lease generalizes the v0.5 one-writer lock from plan scope to
`(repository, path-region)` scope: when an execution starts it reserves the
`allowed_paths` it holds in each repository. While a lease is held, no other
execution that is not a descendant of the holder in the plan-dependency graph may
acquire an overlapping region in that repository (INV-CONCURRENCY-01). This
composes with INV-OWN-01 and turns a would-be merge conflict between unrelated
plans into an ordering decision made before either worker runs.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `wrapper/contracts/schemas/lease.yaml` as the lease record shape stored
under `.runtime/locks/paths/` — holder plan, repository, path region, acquired-at,
released-at. In `wrapper/runtime/engine.sh` implement deterministic
acquire/check/release functions with overlap detection and descendant-exemption
(a declared dependent stacks on or integrates the holder rather than being
blocked). Records are written atomically; a partial record grants nothing
(INV-RUNTIME-02). A competitor is read-only or blocked, never allowed to steal a
live lease.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: a plan's repository set and `allowed_paths`, the plan-dependency graph,
existing lease records. Outputs: lease records under `.runtime/locks/paths/`, and
acquire/check/release decisions (granted, blocked, or read-only) driven by overlap
and descendant rules.

## Risks and open questions

- A lease prevents only a textual conflict on overlapping paths; semantic coupling
  across different files is out of scope and must be modeled as one plan or a
  dependency.
- Lease lifetime runs from execution start until delivery (not merely
  verification), so an unrelated third plan cannot grab the region before verified
  work lands.

## Stop conditions

- Do not expand scope beyond the declared repositories and paths.
