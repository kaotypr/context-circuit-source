## Intended behavior

One independent, read-only verifier inspects the latest worker commit of every
affected repository, replays the declared verification commands and acceptance
evidence from the immutable execution snapshot, and reports each acceptance and
verification id with observed evidence, layer, and a passed/failed/blocked
outcome. The verifier is a separate actor that cannot modify product files, plan
intent, or worker commits. When no independent verifier is available the
execution is blocked, never self-verified.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `wrapper/contracts/schemas/verifier-result.yaml` (the passed/failed/
blocked/waived result shape, per-id evidence, repository and commit checked).
Author `agents/verifier.md` defining the independent read-only role and its
prohibitions. Add verifier-result recording to `wrapper/runtime/engine.sh` that
declares verifier scope and rejects a result whose role includes product writes;
a missing independent verifier yields a blocked execution.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the latest worker commits, the immutable plan snapshot, the worker
handoff as a claim, and declared verification commands. Outputs: a recorded
verifier-result record, or a blocked outcome when read-only verification cannot
be performed.
