## Intended behavior

A mechanical, deterministic grader (no model call) decides a run's pass/fail from
ground-truth traces and read-only inspection across four dimensions: A state
post-conditions, B transcript checks, C access-discipline audit, and D efficiency
ledger. Dimensions A, B, and C's `forbidden`-read check are hard gates; C's
`required`-read misses and the whole of dimension D are soft (warning-only).
Every finding maps back to an acceptance criterion and a
`wrapper/contracts/invariants.yaml` rule.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Implement `template-harness/human/grade.sh` to read the case's `grader:` block,
the instantiated workspace (via read-only `engine.sh` operations, file checks,
and a diff against the pristine baseline), the recorded transcript, and the
file-access trace/telemetry. Dimension A checks state post-conditions (plans not
advanced past draft, commit-before-verify ordering, `done` only after a verifier
pass, Product Knowledge unchanged, identity not fabricated). Dimension B applies
`forbids_regex` and `requires_any` by intent. Dimension C compares the file-access
trace to the per-action `access_policy` at session level (`forbidden`
intersection is the hard gate; `required` misses advisory). Dimension D records
per-action turns/tokens/tool-calls/wall-clock and the process-overhead ratio as
warnings only. The grader keys on invariants and side effects, never exact
wording, and degrades C/D where the host does not expose the trace.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the workspace path (read-only), the transcript, the file-access trace,
optional telemetry, the product host and trace-availability flag, and the case
`grader:` block. Outputs: a per-dimension result, an overall verdict, and a run
summary line with the mapped acceptance criteria and invariants.

## Risks and open questions

- The grader must remain fully deterministic; any LLM use in the pass/fail path
  would contaminate the traces it judges.
- Efficiency numbers are model-dependent and must never fail a run, only
  annotate it (HRN-AC-03).
