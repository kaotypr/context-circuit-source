## Intended behavior

The cc-execute skill maps natural-language "approve", "execute", and "approve
and execute" requests to the approval gate and the execution run. "Approve and
execute" is handled as two sequential, explicit actions rather than one fused
step. The skill refuses to execute an unapproved plan.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author `.agents/skills/cc-execute/SKILL.md`. It routes approval language to the
plan-approve transition (EXE-001) and execution language to execution-begin and
the worker run (EXE-002/EXE-003), invoking the runtime rather than duplicating
its policy. It preserves the read-only `host-blocked` outcome when required
children are unavailable and never self-verifies.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: a plan id and the user's approve/execute intent. Outputs: an approval
transition and/or a started execution, or a refusal when the plan is not
approved.
