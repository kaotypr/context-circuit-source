## Intended behavior

After a plan is marked done, the workspace begins post-implementation knowledge
reconciliation, comparing actual commits and verifier evidence against the
Product Knowledge that grounded the plan. The result is either an explicit
no-update statement or one or more proposals for new, changed, retired, stale, or
conflicting context. Reconciliation never silently accepts any proposal;
acceptance stays a separate explicit human decision.

## Concrete change and affected surfaces

Author the context-impact handoff/proposal shape in
`wrapper/contracts/schemas/context-impact.yaml` and add engine primitives in
`wrapper/runtime/engine.sh` to record the context-impact handoff or proposal
references linked to the implementation completion record. The runtime stores
references only and does not interpret or accept Product Knowledge; a plan may
remain done while its proposals stay pending or deferred.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: the implementation completion record, changed paths and commits per
affected repository, worker handoffs, verifier evidence, and the plan's grounding
context references. Outputs: a recorded context-impact handoff carrying either a
no-update-needed result or proposal references with impact status
(review-needed, deferred, or conflict).
